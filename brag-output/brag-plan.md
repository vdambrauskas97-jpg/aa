# Brag Plan: EasyBid

Source: the live site `easybid.lt` (Lithuanian version, captured 2026-11-11), not this repository's code.
On-screen language: Lithuanian, the site's default. Every product claim and number below is copied from the site.

## What is this app?
EasyBid lets Lithuanian buyers search Copart and IAAI US auction lots, bid through EasyBid's broker account, and see the full price delivered to Klaipėda before they bid; EasyBid then handles pickup, sea freight and customs.

## The angle
The auction price is the bait; the Klaipėda price is the truth. One real lot carries the whole idea: a 2017 Hyundai Accent at **575 USD** on Copart is **3 401 €** once it is in Klaipėda, and EasyBid shows that number, fee by fee, before you bid. The video shows the real price panel doing its job, then the site's own three-step flow: find, bid, receive.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A lot photo fills the frame and one number lands: `575 USD`, labelled `Dabartinė kaina`. Then the question: `O kiek Klaipėdoje?` It is the question every importer has, and the site answers it.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The real `Klaipėdoje / Preliminaru` panel fills in row by row (auction fees, transport, brokerage, duty, VAT, port handling) and lands on the navy bar: `Preliminari galutinė kaina 3 401 €`.
- Search: `bmw m5` typed into the search bar, three rows arrive, each with `Statymas` and `Su pristatymu`. `683 337 lotų`, Copart and IAAI in one list.
- Bid: `Jūsų maksimali suma $22,000`, the bid ticks `$21,300 → $21,600 → $21,900`, then `Lotas laimėtas už $21,900`.
- Delivery: the site's own map, `Newark, US → Klaipėda, LT`, `≈ 6–9 savaitės nuo durų iki durų`, `Muitinė sutvarkyta`.

## Outro / punchline
The logo, the site's own headline `JAV aukcionų automobiliai iki Klaipėdos`, the button `Pradėti statyti` and `easybid.lt`. No joke: the punchline already happened at 3 401 €.

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result, all from the site:
1. Find: one search over Copart and IAAI, with the delivered price in every row.
2. Bid: you name a maximum; EasyBid enters the bid by hand and never exceeds it.
3. Receive: paid, shipped across the Atlantic, cleared through customs in Klaipėda.

The centerpiece is the price panel from a real lot page; the flow scenes rebuild the animated demos from the site's `Kaip tai veikia` page with their own example data (2019 BMW M5).

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: the honest price reveal — a calm, exact product film where one number tells the truth
- Interpretation: confident and precise rather than jokey. Pace comes from the interface working (rows landing, a bid ticking, a ship crossing), type stays still once it lands, and sound is restrained.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (steady and clean, 109.96 BPM)
- Music treatment: start 1.65 s into the track so its quiet opening sits under the hook and the fee rows and its lift lands on the total; bed at 0.5; 0.3 s fade-in, 1.5 s fade-out under the final hold; the finished file is mastered to about -16 LUFS
- Music cue guidance: preset read from `assets/music/cues/…vol-12….music-cues.md`; see the section below
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the glow behind the navy total bar and behind the outro logo breathes with the music's RMS. No waveform or equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse to moderate, motion-matched, quiet; low high-frequency-risk files for anything repeated
- Audio-coupled moments: fee rows landing, the total landing, typed search query, result rows, bid ticks, the win banner, the pin in Klaipėda, the outro logo
- Restraint rule: no sound may be louder than the total landing; repeated ticks stay in the background; nothing on a title that is already still

## Music cue guidance
Track cues are shifted by the 1.65 s start offset, so film time = track time − 1.65.
- Tempo 109.96 BPM, one beat ≈ 0.546 s.
- Strong cues to lock (film time): **7.07 s** total lands (track 8.74, the bass entry measured per frame), **7.43 s** claim line starts, **15.82 s** delivery scene starts (track 17.47), **16.90 s** pin lands in Klaipėda (track 18.56).
- Beat-grid windows: fee rows 3.26–5.19 s, read as texture and then held as a full set; search rows 10.47–10.91 s with the row highlight on the kick at 11.30 s; bid ticks at 13.50 and 13.95 s, win banner on the kick at 14.17 s; outro logo on the kick at 18.80 s.
- Restraint: text reveals never run faster than one line per beat unless the set is then held; readability wins over the grid.

### Scene 2 — Reveal: the price in Klaipėda — 7.07s (2.74–9.81)

### Scene 3 — Find: one search — 2.78s (9.81–12.59)

Audio-coupled idea: key ticks while typing, a soft drop per row
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4 (the highlighted row becomes the bid card for the same car)

### Scene 4 — Bid: never above your maximum — 3.23s (12.59–15.82)
